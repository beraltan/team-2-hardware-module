# Joint and side-stop revision R3 — 28 September 2026

Reprint **Radar Holder** and **Front Cover**. The R2 Electronics Sled and ESP Lock are unchanged and can be reused. The package includes the complete matching set for a new build.

- Cover-to-sled tongue notch: 0.08 mm nominal clearance per side and at the front, reduced from 0.15 mm.
- Sled-to-holder dovetail: 0.10 mm nominal clearance per face, reduced from 0.15 mm.
- Integral closed-end side stop added to the holder's dovetail, with 0.10 mm nominal end clearance. Insert the sled tongue from the open end until it reaches the stop, then fit the front cover. Remove the cover before sliding the tongue back out through the same open end. Do not push it through the closed end.
- R2 ESP fit, thicker/wider battery clips and top/bottom cover catches are retained.

Print the paired `Dovetail - Sled.stl` and `Dovetail - Holder.stl` samples first. The holder sample includes the actual closed stop. These small clearances need a physical fit check with your printer and filament; do not force a binding joint.

All files are millimetres and supplied on the bed. The latch shoulders, latch pockets and PCB fingers still contain small overhangs; inspect the slice and use localized support if required, keeping release slots free.

Verification: all four production meshes and eight fit samples are single connected watertight meshes. The assembled sled/holder/cover have zero pairwise mesh intersection. The stop blocks a 0.3 mm overtravel test; six sampled positions along the open-side withdrawal path are collision-free. CAD sampled guide travel is clear, and the cover retains the joint in both sideways directions. Physical fit and strength remain to be checked.
